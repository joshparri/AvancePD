From the seven sites you listed, only some directly support Avance MSP work.  The rest are either broad personal productivity tools or aimed at your independent business.  Here’s a quick look at each and a recommended order for upgrades:

1. **Avance‑PD (`avance-pd.vercel.app`) – top priority.**

   * It is the day‑to‑day “work OS” for your Avance shifts.  It shows upcoming shifts, helps you start and end them, and includes quick capture for work logs, follow‑ups and tasks.  It also contains the Healthy MSP Shift card and Health & Outdoors module, allowing you to manage your energy and track hydration/outdoor breaks on the job.  Because this app touches nearly every part of your Avance workflow—shift management, task capture, health, repeated‑issue suggestions, backup/export and optional Supabase sync—it gives the biggest return if you improve it further.

2. **Avance Professional Development (`avance-professional-development.vercel.app`) – important but secondary.**

   * This site is more about training and growth than daily operations.  It has the MSP Skills matrix, scenario trainer, quiz engine and learning cockpit.  Upgrading it will continue to sharpen your technical and soft skills, but it won’t replace the operational gaps in Avance‑PD.  Once Avance‑PD is solid, any improvements here (new scenarios, better quizzing, integrated feedback) will directly boost your capabilities.

3. **Field Notes (`field-notes-two.vercel.app`) – integrate or fold into Avance‑PD.**

   * This single‑purpose app lets you record incident notes (“issue, what you checked, what you changed, result”) and export them.  While useful, it overlaps with Avance‑PD’s quick‑capture notes and work‑log features.  If you upgrade Avance‑PD to offer a dedicated “incident note” template, you may not need to maintain a separate Field Notes app.

4. **ParrisTechApp (`parris-tech-app.vercel.app`) and ParrisTechServicesApp (`parris-tech-services-app.vercel.app`) – lower priority for Avance.**

   * ParrisTechApp is a guided tech‑checkup tool with client profiles, session timers, checklists and invoice lines; ParrisTechServicesApp adds appointment booking, client management, invoicing and reconciliation.  These are powerful for your freelance/consulting work but not directly aligned with Avance, which already uses Halo for ticketing and invoicing.  Upgrading them benefits your side business, not your Avance duties.

5. **Waypoint (`waypoint-azure.vercel.app`) and JoshHub (`josh-hub-two.vercel.app`) – optional.**

   * Waypoint focuses on daily anchors, wellbeing guardrails and downtime loops.  JoshHub is an all‑purpose life OS that tracks faith, family, finances and health.  They support your personal growth and holistic wellbeing but don’t drive Avance‑specific outcomes.  Minor upgrades here can wait until after the core work apps are solid.

### Recommendation

If your goal is to get the most impact on your Avance hours, **prioritise upgrading Avance‑PD first**: polish the shift‑ and task‑flow, integrate playbooks and checklists, and improve note capture and health prompts.  Next, enhance **Avance Professional Development** for deeper skill and scenario training.  Evaluate whether **Field Notes** should be merged into Avance‑PD.  The remaining apps (ParrisTechApp, ParrisTechServicesApp, Waypoint, JoshHub) are valuable for other contexts but don’t need immediate attention for your Avance role.
