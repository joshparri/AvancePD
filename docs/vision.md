# Avance Work Companion Vision

Avance Work Companion is a local-first support and professional development app for Josh's ICT/MSP work rhythm at Avance.

## Purpose

- Help Josh start shifts calmly and quickly.
- Keep generic work context, follow-ups, learning, and evidence visible.
- Support sustainable Monday/Wednesday MSP work without storing client-sensitive material.
- Build practical MSP confidence through scenarios, micro-learning, ticket-note practice, communication practice, and manager-safe evidence summaries.

## Product Principles

- Local-first by default.
- Fast capture over perfect documentation.
- Gentle prompts, not shame.
- Professional wording suitable for workplace use.
- Privacy before convenience.
- Practical PD that connects learning to real support habits.
- Calm, readable UI that works under pressure.

## Privacy Rules

Do not store:

- client names or company names
- passwords, API keys, recovery codes, or secrets
- IP addresses, hostnames, screenshots, copied ticket text, or private operational details
- private health diagnoses or medical notes

Use generic categories such as "M365 access request", "endpoint issue", "triage queue", or "documentation follow-up".

## Current Scope

- Dashboard
- Avance Workday
- Health & Outdoors
- Shift scheduler
- Tasks
- Work logs
- Knowledge
- Playbooks
- Time
- PD learning log
- MSP skills, scenarios, quiz, roadmap, ticket notes, communication practice, micro-learning, and Evidence Pack

## Success Metrics

- Josh can understand the next useful action within 60 seconds of opening the app.
- Busy-shift capture takes less than a minute.
- Follow-ups survive the gap between Monday and Wednesday shifts.
- Learning evidence is manager-safe and useful without revealing sensitive details.
- Health prompts support sustainability without interrupting urgent ticket work.
