# UI, UX, And Tone Guide

The app should feel calm, practical, and work-ready.

## Tone

- Brief and direct.
- Warm without being overly casual.
- No shame, guilt, or red failure language for normal misses.
- Prefer "reset", "review", "next action", and "keep going steadily".
- Keep faith prompts optional and workplace-appropriate.

## UX Principles

- Surface the next useful action quickly.
- Keep dashboards scannable.
- Use cards for repeated items and focused tools.
- Keep forms compact and clear.
- Preserve keyboard access.
- Use large enough tap targets for mobile.
- Avoid clutter during busy work states.

## Visual Principles

- High contrast text.
- Calm neutral surfaces.
- Clear headings.
- Consistent status chips.
- No red states for skipped health or learning actions.
- Support reduced motion.

## Accessibility Checklist

- Buttons and inputs are keyboard reachable.
- Focus states are visible.
- Headings follow a sensible order.
- Text does not depend on colour alone.
- Mobile layout remains usable without horizontal scrolling.
